# National Banana Registry - Analysis Engine

## Methodology & Documentation

The Banana Analysis Engine is an entirely client-side computer vision pipeline built specifically for the 18-hour hackathon constraints. To satisfy the requirement for *real computation* while avoiding backend dependency bloat, the pipeline is implemented in the browser using **OpenCV.js** (WebAssembly).

All measurements generated by this engine are strict **image-derived estimations** based on heuristic computer vision techniques. They are *not* laboratory-calibrated physical measurements, and the UI accurately reflects this distinction to maintain technical honesty.

---

### Pipeline Stages

#### 1. Banana Detection & Segmentation (Heuristic)
- **Method**: Color Space Thresholding (HSV).
- **Process**: The image is downscaled for performance, converted from RGB to HSV space, and thresholded using a heuristic range specifically tuned for Yellow/Green/Brown (H: 15-90, S: 40-255, V: 40-255).
- **Refinement**: Morphological OPEN and CLOSE operations clean up noise and holes in the resulting mask.
- **Validation**: `cv.findContours` runs on the mask. The largest contour is selected. If the bounding area is below 5000px, it throws an `INSUFFICIENT_QUALITY` error.

#### 2. Curvature Methodology
The primary technical feature of Step 3 is calculating the banana's curvature.
- **Method**: Geometric deviation from a linear axis.
- **Process**:
  1. The engine scans the contour boundary to find the two extreme points furthest apart (the tips).
  2. A straight line is virtually drawn connecting these two tips.
  3. The engine iterates over the boundary points to find the maximum orthogonal deviation from that straight line.
- **Metrics**:
  - **Curvature Angle**: Approximated using the ratio `(Max Deviation / Tip Distance) * 180`.
  - **Straightness Index**: `100 - (deviation_ratio * 300)`.
- **Classification Thresholds**:
  - `> 45°`: EXTREMELY CURVED
  - `> 30°`: HIGHLY CURVED
  - `> 15°`: MODERATELY CURVED
  - `> 5°`: SLIGHTLY CURVED
  - `< 5°`: VERY STRAIGHT

#### 3. Ripeness Estimation
- **Method**: Average Hue Analysis.
- **Process**: The HSV image is masked by the segmentation contour. The mean Hue value of the segmented area is calculated.
- **Logic**:
  - `Hue > 35`: Stage 2 — Green-Yellow
  - `Hue > 25`: Stage 4 — Yellow
  - `Hue < 25`: Stage 6 — Overripe/Brown-dominant
- **Output**: Mapped into an artificial `0-100%` Ripeness Score for the dashboard.

#### 4. Banana Condition Score
- **Method**: Project-defined composite heuristic.
- **Logic**: It starts at 100. It deducts points for extremes in ripeness (too green or too brown) and extremes in curvature (too straight or too curled).
- **Transparency**: This is explicitly documented as a "Banana Condition Score" composite, not an agricultural standard.

---

### Known Limitations
1. **No Real-World Calibration**: Because no reference coin or ruler is required in the photograph, all distance measurements are in image-relative units. Thus, no centimetre measurements are falsely claimed.
2. **Lighting Sensitivity**: Strong blue lighting or extreme shadows may fail the HSV thresholding step, resulting in a false "No banana detected" error.
3. **2D Projection**: The curvature algorithm operates on a 2D photograph. If a banana is photographed pointing directly at the camera, its curvature will appear as 0°.
4. **OpenCV WebAssembly Load Time**: The initial load of the `opencv.js` script may take a few seconds on slower connections, handled gracefully by the UI's loading states.
